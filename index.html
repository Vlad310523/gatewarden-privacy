Gatewarden Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Gatewarden ("the game", "we") handles. The game is a single-player offline tower defense game. It has no user accounts and we do not run any servers that receive your data.

Information the game stores on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, upgrades, settings such as music and sound volume, and your endless-mode records) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads appear only between battles or when you choose to watch a video for a reward. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crash logs of the ads SDK.

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch the game shows Google's consent form. You can decide whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings, where a privacy options button is shown when it applies to you.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • If you do not want any data processed by the ads SDK, you can stop using the game and uninstall it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone. The only third party that receives data from the game is Google AdMob, as described above.

Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Connections made by the ads SDK are encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
